09 · Companion badge: symbols

Both icons share the teal ground and the white mark; only the doctor icon carries the badge. All symbols use the system’s line-icon style (2px, round caps). Each is shown at 140, 60 and 29px.

Badge styles (stethoscope; plus and pulse at 60px)

Position and size

Notes

  • At 29px only bold, simple symbols survive: the plus, the pulse and “Dr” stay readable; the stethoscope, bag and clipboard turn into a dot.
  • The stethoscope says “doctor” most directly; the plus reads as “medical” but is close to generic health apps; “Dr” is clearest but puts text in the icon, which the stores advise against.
  • The calendar and heart symbols describe features rather than the audience, so they may confuse people choosing between the two apps.
  • The charcoal disc with the teal ring separates best from the teal ground; the glass disc is elegant but low-contrast.
