SECTION 7 · RECOMMENDED CHANGES · 30 OCT 2026

What to change next

Product and design changes beyond the fixes, ordered by impact. None of these is built in the prototype yet.

High impactCALENDAR · WEB

Make the week view the default on desktop and add a Month view

  • Open the calendar on Week (today in the first third of the grid) on screens ≥ 1100; keep Day for phones. The month picker becomes a collapsible side panel, freeing ~300px for the grid.
  • Add Month as a third view in the Day / Week toggle (dots per day, click a day to open Day).
  • Drag on empty time to create an appointment; drag an event to move it; drag its bottom edge to change the length, with an Undo toast.
  • Label the two location lanes inside a day (e.g. a thin header "Clinic · Private") or merge them and colour the event edge by location.
  • Show a working-hours scroll position (start at the first working hour) and a compact all-day row for days off and blocks.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move between days, N creates, T goes to today.

MediumCHARTS

Make charts keyboard and screen-reader accessible

  • Bars and donut segments become focusable with a tooltip on focus.
  • Every chart gets a "View as table" toggle (DataTable) and a one-line text summary.
MediumCOMPONENTS

Turn the remaining compositions into DS classes

  • 22 components are built correctly from DS parts and tokens but have no class of their own yet: AiCopilot, AiInsightCard, AppointmentTimeline, Banner, ChartCard, ChatAttachments, ChatBubble, ConsultationSection, ConversationList, DocumentTile, DocumentViewer, FilterBar, KpiCard, NextPatientHero, NotificationCenter, PageHeader, PageToolbar, PatientHeader, ScheduleEditor, Section, Tag, TaskCard.
  • Start with KpiCard, EmptyState, PageToolbar and ChatBubble: they repeat most and are shared with the patient app.
MediumBRAND COLOUR

White labels on the teal primary

  • The primary is teal-50 again in both apps (Nov 2026). White labels on it are 2.4:1 and fail WCAG AA.
  • If AA becomes a requirement, change one token: a darker teal fill, or charcoal labels on the teal.
MediumNAVIGATION · WEB

Global search and quick create

  • Make the sidebar search a command palette (⌘K): patients, appointments, actions.
  • One "New" button in the sidebar (appointment, patient, plan, task) so creating is never more than one click away.
LowFORMS

Protect unsaved work

  • Ask before leaving a form with unsaved changes (the ActionBar already knows the state).
  • Autosave drafts of visit notes every few seconds with a "Saved" line in the ActionBar.
LowPHONE

Small phone polish

  • Truncate long names in the calendar agenda to one line.
  • Collapse the FAB to its icon after scrolling one screen, as the DS spec allows.
  • Pull to refresh on lists instead of refresh buttons.
