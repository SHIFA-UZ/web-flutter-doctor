APP ICON EXPLORATION · NOV 2026

Shifa and Shifa Doctor: nine treatments of one mark

Every pair uses the same Shifa “S” mark and only design-system colours. The patient app keeps the brand teal; the doctor app is told apart by a dark ground (or, in 09, a badge), so the two read as a family on one home screen. Shaders are real: SVG noise, lighting and displacement filters, CSS blur and backdrop blur.

01

Solid

60 · 40 · 29

The baseline. Patient: the white mark on brand teal. Doctor: the white mark on charcoal, with the mark’s two dots in teal.

Clearest at 29px. The teal dots turn the logo’s own detail into the doctor signal, with no extra symbol.

02

Liquid glass

60 · 40 · 29

Blurred brand blobs under a frosted glass tile (white 25%, backdrop blur), the same glass as the splash and the nav bar.

Matches iOS 26 Liquid Glass. The tile edge softens below 40px.

03

Mesh gradient

60 · 40 · 29

Three radial light sources over teal (patient) or media black (doctor), with a soft shadow under the mark.

Calm and premium. Doctor reads as the “night” version of the same app.

04

Aurora blur

60 · 40 · 29

A conic sweep of teal, blue and white, blurred 30px and rotated, then veiled.

Most colourful. Blue is a status colour in the system, so it is the riskiest for brand.

05

Grain shader

60 · 40 · 29

A diagonal gradient with a fractal-noise grain overlay (SVG feTurbulence, overlay blend).

Tactile, print-like texture. Grain disappears at small sizes, which is fine.

06

Glow

60 · 40 · 29

Patient: a white mark with a soft bloom. Doctor: a teal mark glowing out of the dark, built from a blurred copy behind it.

Striking on dark home screens. The teal mark on dark breaks today’s rule (white mark only).

07

Embossed

60 · 40 · 29

The mark lit as a raised surface (specular lighting filter) over a lit gradient.

Feels physical, close to Apple’s 2025 icon lighting. Busier at small sizes.

08

Liquid flow

60 · 40 · 29

Brand-colour bands distorted by a noise displacement shader, like ink in water.

Most expressive. Each export is unique (seeded noise), so pin the seed.

09

Companion badge

60 · 40 · 29

Both apps share the teal icon; the doctor icon adds a charcoal medical-plus badge in the corner.

Shows the two apps belong together. The badge is a new symbol next to the logo, which some stores discourage.

Notes for choosing

  • Corners here are a rounded square at 22.4% radius; the stores apply their own mask (iOS squircle, Android adaptive circle or squircle), so export full-bleed squares.
  • The design system says the white mark sits only on brand teal or the glass tile. Directions 01, 05, 07 colour its dots, and 06 makes the mark teal on dark: whichever you pick needs that rule extended.
  • On a home screen the dark doctor icons separate best from the patient icon (see the next board). 09 keeps both teal, so they are hardest to tell apart at a glance.
  • Shader directions (04, 05, 07, 08) need a flat fallback for tiny sizes (notifications, settings): 01 Solid works as that fallback for any of them.
