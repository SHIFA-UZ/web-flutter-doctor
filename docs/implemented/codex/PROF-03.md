**Answers:**
1. *Reopen closes at once (P1):* fixed; the screen consumes "done" (`closed()`) before it closes, so a reopened editor never sees the old value. Tested.
2. *Saving after an upload brings back the cached photo:* fixed at the root; `ProfileRepository` remembers the last upload's plain and versioned URL and swaps the plain one back on every later write (refresh, PATCH answers). Tested.
3. *iOS decodes the full photo:* fixed; gallery photos are decoded by ImageIO straight to ≤ 1600 px (`CGImageSourceCreateThumbnailAtIndex` with the EXIF transform). Camera photos arrive from UIKit already decoded. Compiles; not run on an iPhone yet.
4. *Mirrored EXIF orientations:* fixed; all eight orientations are applied.
5. *Permanent camera denial:* fixed; a refusal (now or earlier) shows "Camera access is needed to take a photo" with Open settings (Android app details page, iOS app settings). iOS checks `AVCaptureDevice` first, since UIKit's picker shows a black screen without access. Checked on the emulator.
6. *No square crop:* not built. Android has no platform cropper, so this needs a new dependency (uCrop or similar) or a custom crop screen. Flutter didn't crop either, and the avatar is displayed center-cropped. Left for the user to decide; recorded in profile.md.
7. *Bundled list waits for the network:* fixed; the bundled catalogue shows at once and the backend's replaces it when it answers. Tested with a slow backend.
8. *Legacy professions can't be unticked:* fixed in the shared picker (registration too); a saved profession missing from the catalogue is a ticked "Current: …" row on top.
